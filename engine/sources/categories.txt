# The tabs of the "Add a source" panel, in order. Each is a folder in engine/sources/: put a new
# source's file in the folder it belongs to and run ./build.sh (tools/gen_registry.py makes the
# registry from the folders). A tab with nothing in it yet still shows, with a note.
#
# folder       | tab label        | what goes here
time           | Time & tempo     | LFOs, sequencers, clocks, tempo-synced shapes
playing        | Your playing     | envelopes, pick triggers, pitch and dynamics followers
chaos          | Physics & chaos  | orbits, random walks, simulations
outside        | Outside world    | controllers, sensors, the body
