# The tabs of the "Add an effect" panel, in order. Each is a folder in engine/modules/: put a new
# effect's file in the folder it belongs to and run ./build.sh (tools/gen_registry.py makes the
# registry from the folders). A tab with nothing in it yet still shows, with a note.
#
# folder       | tab label     | what goes here
dynamics       | Dynamics      | gates, compressors, limiters
drive          | Amps & drive  | amp captures, overdrive, distortion, fuzz
cab            | Cabs & IRs    | speaker cabinets, impulse responses
filter         | Filter & EQ   | EQs, wahs, filters
synth          | Synth filters | filters from classic synths: ladders, state-variable, diode, Sallen-Key, comb, formant
modulation     | Modulation    | chorus, flanger, phaser, tremolo, vibrato
pitch          | Pitch         | octaves, harmonies, pitch shifting
delay          | Delay         | echoes and repeats
reverb         | Reverb        | rooms, halls, plates, spaces
utility        | Utility       | volume, splits, tools
experimental   | Experimental  | anything that doesn't fit a box
